Human Made

Human Made is a Japanese lifestyle brand that launched in 2010. The brand is based on the idea that "The Future is in the Past". It combines vintage and street culture with unique graphics and excellent craftsmanship.

Nike

Nike, Inc. is an American athletic footwear and apparel corporation headquartered near Beaverton, Oregon, United States. It is the world's largest supplier of athletic shoes and apparel and a major manufacturer of sports equipment.

New Balance

New Balance is a multinational corporation that manufactures sports footwear and apparel. Founded in 1906 as the New Balance Arch Support Company. New Balance is known for its quality and innovation, and uses technologies like Fresh Foam and FuelCell to provide cushioning and support.

ThisIsNeverThat

ThisIsNeverThat is a Seoul-based fashion brand that was founded in 2018. The brand's designs are inspired by 90s and early 2000s streetwear, and include typography-heavy t-shirts, hoodies, and sweats, as well as Pertex down jackets.
